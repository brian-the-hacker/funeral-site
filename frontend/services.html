Our services

Complete funeral services under one roof, from the first call to the farewell.

Mortuary and refrigeration

Our facility offers body storage and preservation, with ultra-modern refrigeration. We also provide embalming and preparation of bodies.

Body transfer

We transfer the deceased from the place of death to the mortuary. Call us any time and we will arrange collection.

International repatriation

We specialise in the dignified repatriation of human remains, both returning loved ones from overseas and sending them abroad. It is a highly regulated process governed by aviation law, public health rules, embassy requirements and airline policies.

We manage the legal, medical, cultural and logistical steps so your family does not have to, and we keep you informed throughout.

Hearse services

We have several modern hearses, available for transport nationwide. All our hearses come with:

  • Lowering gear with grass mats
  • P.A. system
  • Gazebo and executive church trolley

Coffin manufacturing

We manufacture coffins and can build custom designs. Contact us to see the models available.

Exhumation

We carry out exhumations in liaison with the relevant authorities.

Memorial items and funeral planning

We supply floral wreaths and memorial items, coordinate the burial and funeral ceremony, and offer funeral insurance and planning support.
