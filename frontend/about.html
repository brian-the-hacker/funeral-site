About us

Our mission, our values, and the people behind the funeral home.

Our mission

To provide compassionate, transparent and professional funeral services that honour the dignity of every individual.

Our vision

To be a leading provider of mortuary and funeral services, offering innovation, integrity and unmatched care.

Our core values

Compassion and empathy

We support grieving families with patience and respect.

Professionalism and ethics

Staff are trained continuously to uphold professional standards.

Integrity and transparency

Clear pricing and open communication at every stage.

Innovation and dynamism

Modern facilities and methods, always improving.

Respect for tradition

We honour every family’s cultural and religious customs.

Our charter

Ethical and operational standards

  • Dignity and respect in all handling of remains
  • Transparency in pricing and communication
  • Strict hygiene, PPE and public health standards
  • Confidentiality of all client information

Health and safety

  • Sanitary mortuary facilities with backup power
  • Safe use of chemicals, equipment and refrigeration
  • Routine safety inspections
  • Biohazard disposal protocols

Support for families

  • Guidance through documents, permits and burial processes
  • Open, compassionate communication throughout
  • Grief support referrals when needed

Governance

  • Legal compliance and regular quality reviews
  • Proper documentation and secure records
  • Feedback welcomed from families and the community

Our history

  1. 2009Makueni Pamoja Association starts as a group of people committed to development. Francis Mutisya is its first chairperson.
  2. 2015The funeral home begins operating on plots the group bought.
  3. 2019Cosmas Maweu becomes chairperson. Charles Mutunga is vice chairperson and managing director of the funeral home.

Our aim has always been to provide quality funeral services to the people of Makueni, Kenya and beyond.
